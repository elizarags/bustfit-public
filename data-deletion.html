Deleting your data

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version: bustfit is made by Bust Fit Labs LLC. We delete what we store from your Instagram comments and messages automatically after 90 days. To have it deleted sooner, send us a direct message on Instagram or an email, and we'll delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

1. Instagram data

If you commented on or messaged bustfit's Instagram account, @trybustfit, we may have stored your Instagram username and ID, your comment or message text, and records of the automatic message we sent you and whether you tapped its link. Section 18 of our Privacy Policy explains exactly what and why. We delete our copy automatically 90 days after we receive it. Our connection to Instagram runs through Zernio, which keeps its own copy under its own privacy policy.

To have it deleted sooner, either:

  • Send a direct message to @trybustfit on Instagram saying "delete my data", or
  • Email hello@bustfit.com with your Instagram username and the subject "Delete my Instagram data"

We'll delete everything we stored about your Instagram account within 30 days, ask Zernio to delete its copy, and reply to confirm when it's done. We may need to check that the request really comes from you, for example by replying in Instagram's direct messages.

Comments you posted on our Instagram posts are stored by Instagram, not by us. You can delete those yourself in Instagram at any time.

2. The bustfit email list

Use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or email us, and we'll remove your address. See Section 17 of our Privacy Policy.

3. The bustfit Chrome extension

Almost everything the extension stores stays on your own device. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes it. For the few things sent to our servers, such as problem reports and feedback from the panel, email us with the approximate date and the product and we'll locate and remove them. See Section 13 of our Privacy Policy.
